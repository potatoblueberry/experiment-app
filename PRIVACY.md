# N1 Lab – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

N1 Lab is a personal, non-commercial project under active development. It helps a user run
self-experiments (N-of-1 trials) on their own wearable data.

## What data the app handles

- **Wearable data** you explicitly authorise through a provider such as Oura: daily sleep,
  readiness, activity, heart-rate and HRV summaries, and temperature deviation.
- **Data you type in**: experiment descriptions, daily yes/no adherence answers and
  optional journal notes.
- **Account data** needed to sign you in (an e-mail address).

## How it is used

Only to show you your own trends and to compute the results of your own experiments.
Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## Where it is stored

During development all data stays on your own device. When a backend is enabled, data is
stored in a database that only your account can read (row-level security). Provider access
tokens are stored server-side only and are never exposed to the app.

## Your control

You can disconnect a wearable provider at any time, which stops further syncing, and you
can delete your account and all associated data on request.
